Payment terms

Last updated: 21 November 2026

1. What is free and what is paid

Ondelio is free to download. For free you can scan, preview, recover up to 500 MB in total to check that it works, restore from “Look here”, copy a drive to an image, diagnose damaged files and securely erase files and free space.

Paid, as add-ons: unlimited recovery, saving repaired files, drive-to-drive copy, full-drive erase and forensic mode. There may be a bundle that includes them all.

2. Where and how you pay

  • All purchases are made inside the Microsoft Store, with the payment methods of your Microsoft account.
  • Microsoft collects the payment. The owner of Ondelio never receives or stores your payment details.
  • The price is shown in the Store in your currency before you confirm, with applicable taxes included. Microsoft issues the receipt.

3. What you buy

  • Each add-on is a licence to use that feature, tied to your Microsoft account, unless the Store states otherwise (for example, a subscription).
  • The licence works offline once checked, and is kept if you reinstall the program with the same account.
  • Before buying, the program shows you what it can recover or repair, so you pay knowing the result.

4. Price changes

Prices may change. A change never affects what you have already bought.

5. Payment problems

Charges, payment methods and invoices are managed by Microsoft from your account (account.microsoft.com/billing/orders). If you have a problem with a feature you paid for, write to gabriel@jurilia.com and we will help.
