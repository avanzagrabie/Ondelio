Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short: Ondelio runs on your computer. It does not upload your files or the contents of your drives to any server, it has no ads and it does not sell or share data. Purchases are handled by Microsoft.

1. Data controller

Gabriel Díaz Bernal, Tax ID 28796685H, Calle Jesús del Gran Poder, 5, 1.º K, 41002 Sevilla (España). Email: gabriel@jurilia.com.

2. What the program does with your data

  • Contents of your drives and files. To scan, preview, recover, repair, copy or erase, Ondelio reads your drives and files only on your computer. That content is never sent anywhere.
  • Settings and sessions. Your preferences, saved scans and the free-recovery counter are stored in folders on your own computer. You can delete them by uninstalling the program or deleting those folders.
  • No telemetry. Ondelio does not send usage statistics. If crash reports are added in the future, they will only be sent with your explicit consent, without the content of your files, and this policy will be updated first.
  • Works offline. The program works without internet. It only connects, through Windows, to check your purchases and updates in the Microsoft Store.
  • On-device artificial intelligence (Ondelio Sense). The program includes a small classification model that examines fragments of the drive on your computer to recognise their type (text, photo, compressed data, program). It sends nothing to any server, does not learn from or store your files, and makes no decisions about you: it only helps find and sort files. It was trained exclusively on public files under open licences or in the public domain, never on users' files.

3. Data processed by Microsoft

Downloads, purchases, payments, licences and updates go through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes that data as controller under its privacy statement. The owner of Ondelio does not receive your payment details; Microsoft only provides aggregated sales reports and, where applicable, the information needed to handle a refund.

4. If you write to us

If you contact us by email, we will use your address and what you tell us only to reply and provide support, based on your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in helping you. We keep the conversation as long as needed to resolve it and for no more than two years, unless the law requires otherwise. Please do not send personal files unless needed.

5. This website

This website uses no cookies, has no forms or analytics and loads no third-party content. It only stores in your browser the language you choose, if you change it. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical connection data such as your IP address for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

6. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability of your data by writing to gabriel@jurilia.com. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) or your local authority.

7. If you live outside the European Union

  • United Kingdom: the UK GDPR applies with the same rights; you can complain to the ICO.
  • United States (including California, CCPA/CPRA): we do not sell or share personal information, do not use it for advertising and do not collect sensitive personal information. You can ask to know what data we hold, correct it or delete it by writing to gabriel@jurilia.com; we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
  • Other countries: if the law of your country gives you more rights over your data, those rights apply.

If you write to us from outside the EU, your email is handled by email providers that may be located in other countries, with the safeguards required by the GDPR.

8. Children

Ondelio is not aimed at children under 14 (13 in the United States) and does not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version here with its date. If the change is significant, we will also announce it in the program.
