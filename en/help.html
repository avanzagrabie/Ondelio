First things first

  • Stop using the drive where the files were. Everything you save can overwrite them.
  • Do not install anything on that drive, not even Ondelio. If it is drive C:, recover to another drive or a USB stick.
  • If the drive makes noises or is very slow, make a copy first with “The drive is failing” and recover from the copy.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see what can be recovered before paying?

Yes. Scanning and previewing are free, and you can recover up to 500 MB for free to check that it works.

Why don't files I deleted from an SSD show up?

On most modern SSDs, Windows tells the drive when files are deleted (TRIM) and the drive clears those areas. It is a physical limitation, not a limitation of the program. Try “Look here” first: the Recycle Bin, previous versions or the cloud may have it.

Can Ondelio damage my drive?

Not when recovering: the scanned drive is opened read-only. Only drive-to-drive copy and full-drive erase write, and always to another drive that you confirm by typing a text; never to the system drive.

Do my files leave my computer?

No. Everything happens on your computer. See the privacy policy.

Which systems are supported?

64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11. NTFS, FAT and exFAT drives and partitions, USB sticks, memory cards and disk images.
