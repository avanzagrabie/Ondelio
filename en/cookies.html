Cookie policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This website uses no cookies or tracking pixels, has no analytics and loads no third-party content. That is why we show no cookie banner.

If you change the language with the menu button, your browser remembers that choice (local storage) to show the website in that language next time. It never leaves your browser and you can delete it from your browser settings. It is strictly necessary for the function you request, so it does not require consent.

The host (GitHub Pages) may log technical connection data for security, as explained in the privacy policy.

The Ondelio program does not use cookies either.
