Refund policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

We want you to pay only for something that works for you. That is why the program shows you, before you buy, what can be recovered or repaired and with what verdict.

1. Who handles refunds

Purchases are made in the Microsoft Store, and Microsoft collects the payment and issues refunds. Refunds are requested from Microsoft through your order history (account.microsoft.com/billing/orders) and follow the Microsoft Store refund policy.

2. Right of withdrawal (European Union)

As an EU consumer you generally have 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. For digital content, that right is lost once the download or use begins with your prior consent and your acknowledgement that you lose it, as you are informed when buying in the Microsoft Store.

3. When we will support your request

Even if withdrawal no longer applies, we will support your refund request to Microsoft if:

  • the feature you bought does not work on your computer because of a bug and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time;
  • you were charged twice or bought by mistake an add-on you already had;
  • the program told you a file was fully recoverable or repairable and the result was not.

Write to gabriel@jurilia.com with the purchase date, the add-on and what happened. You do not need to send us your files.

4. When a refund does not apply

A refund does not apply when the program had already warned you that the data was not recoverable (for example, overwritten or on an SSD with TRIM) or marked it as uncertain or damaged and you still decided to buy, except where the law or Microsoft's policy provides otherwise.

5. Outside the European Union

In the United States, the United Kingdom and all other countries, refunds also follow the Microsoft Store refund policy for your country, and we will support you in the same cases listed in section 3.

6. Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit the legal guarantee of conformity or any other right you have under the consumer law of your country.
